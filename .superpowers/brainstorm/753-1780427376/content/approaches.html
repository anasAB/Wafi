Three approaches for the credit ledger

All include: "آجل" in POS checkout, customer list, invoice tracking, payment recording. The difference is payment flexibility and scope.

A

Core — Full-invoice payments only

Customer pays invoices in full. No partial payments. Simpler model: each invoice is either open or closed.

Pros

  • Faster to build
  • Simpler UI
  • Easier to understand

Cons

  • Doesn't match reality — customers often pay partial amounts
  • Big invoices stay "open" forever
B

Flexible — Partial payments per invoice ⭐ Recommended

Customer can pay any amount against specific invoices. Each invoice tracks total owed vs total paid. Balance = sum of all remaining amounts.

Pros

  • Matches how Syrian shops actually work
  • No stuck open invoices
  • Accurate running balance

Cons

  • Slightly more complex payment modal

⭐ Best fit for the brother's shop and demo

C

Full — Partial payments + manual debt entries

Everything in B, plus ability to manually add a debt not tied to a POS sale (for pre-existing balances when migrating from paper).

Pros

  • Handles migration from paper ledger
  • More complete on day 1

Cons

  • More schema + UI work
  • Manual debts can be added in v1 once ledger is proven
