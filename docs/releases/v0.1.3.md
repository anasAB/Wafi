## Changes
- **Revert v0.1.2's `devices` self-registration gate — it caused a hard
  login deadlock in production.** v0.1.2 made `ensureDeviceRegistered()`
  skip registration unless the JWT's `active_role` claim was already
  `'owner'`, to stop a spurious `devices` 403 right after sign-in. That
  created a circular dependency: `switch_active_operator`
  (`045_switch_active_operator.sql:32-38`) requires the `devices` row to
  already exist server-side, but `active_role` only becomes `'owner'`
  *after* `switch_active_operator` succeeds. For any device without an
  already-registered `deviceCode` (confirmed live: PIN entry sent
  `p_device_id: ""` to the RPC, which fails to cast to `uuid` and gets
  misreported as the generic "needs connectivity" message even with a
  working connection), registration could now never happen at all —
  fully blocking login on that device. Reverted in full
  (`device.store.ts`, `useOperatorSwitch.ts`, and their tests) back to
  v0.1.1's behavior.

## Known issues
- The spurious `devices` 403 noise v0.1.2 tried to fix is back (it was
  cosmetic — dead-lettered, not deadlocking). A real fix needs to
  restructure device self-registration around the actual constraint:
  `devices` INSERT is owner-only RLS (`055_identity_domain_rls.sql`),
  but `switch_active_operator` needs the device row to exist *before*
  `active_role` can become `'owner'`. The working models already in this
  codebase are `bootstrap_owner_identity()` (069) — a `SECURITY DEFINER`
  RPC that creates the first device row bypassing RLS entirely — which
  suggests the fix is a similar dedicated RPC for subsequent-device
  self-registration, not a client-side RLS-gated INSERT. Not yet
  scheduled as a ticket.
- ADR-010's PowerSync sync-rule role-branching gap remains open (tracked
  separately as WAFI-201) and is unrelated to this release's fixes.
